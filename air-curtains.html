An invisible barrier of high-velocity air across the doorway: dust, fumes, heat and insects stay outside, and the cooling you paid for stays inside.

Key benefits

  • Keeps outside air out — dust, fumes and insects blocked
  • Maintains indoor comfort — cool or warm air retained
  • Saves energy — reduces HVAC load and running cost
  • Improves hygiene — fewer airborne pollutants indoors
  • Low noise — powerful but quiet in operation

Features

  • High-velocity, uniform air flow across the full opening
  • Multiple speed settings for different seasons
  • Sleek, compact design that suits the interior
  • Quick installation and hassle-free upkeep
  • Suits single, double, sliding, automatic and revolving doors
  • Sizes to match any door width and height
