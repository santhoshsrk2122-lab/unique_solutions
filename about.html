Seven years of getting buildings wired properly

Unique Solutions is a one-stop security, safety and communication contractor working out of Tamil Nadu, with our own technicians covering five states.

How we got here

We have been in the security and surveillance industry since 2018. In seven-plus years we have built a reputation for first-class service across Tamil Nadu, Bangalore, Pondicherry, Kerala and Andhra.

As a one-stop solution provider, our team of experienced and qualified technicians has installed and maintained more than 900 systems — from a single shop camera to GPS tracking rolled out across 20 districts during a state election.

The reason clients call us back is boring and practical: one contractor holds the whole job, so the cabling, the panel, the configuration and the handover are one person's responsibility.
