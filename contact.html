How a job runs

Five steps from your first call to the annual service visit.

  1. Site survey

    We walk the building, measure the openings and cable routes, and ask what has failed before. No charge, no obligation.

  2. System design

    You get a drawing, a bill of materials with named brands and models, and a price — so you can compare it with anyone else's.

  3. Installation

    Our own technicians run the cabling, mount the devices and terminate the panels, on a schedule agreed before we start.

  4. Testing & handover

    Every device tested, the software configured, your staff trained on it, and the test records handed over for inspection.

  5. Annual maintenance

    Scheduled checks under an AMC, plus emergency call-outs. Fire and suppression systems get the statutory routine on time.
