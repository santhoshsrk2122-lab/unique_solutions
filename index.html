Why sites keep us on the drawing

Most of our work comes from buildings we already wired, and from the people who moved to a new one.

  • One team owns the whole job

    The camera contractor does not blame the network contractor, because they are the same people. Cabling, panels, configuration and handover run on one schedule.

  • Built to pass inspection

    Fire detection and suppression are designed to national and international standards and installed by certified technicians, with the drawings and test records to show for it.

  • Brands you can still get parts for

    Hikvision, Bosch, Honeywell, Cisco, Dahua, CP Plus, eSSL, ZKTeco, Matrix and more — genuine stock, so a replacement board is a phone call, not a redesign.

  • Service after the invoice

    Annual maintenance contracts, scheduled checks and emergency call-outs across Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Bengaluru, Kerala and Andhra.
